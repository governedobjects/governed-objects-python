1. When this runtime implements a capability defined by the Governed
   Objects Specification, including its optional profiles, that capability

3. Features in the open-source edition are not moved to a paid-only
   edition. Paid offerings add value beyond the specification, such as
   hosting, enterprise administration, integrations and support.
